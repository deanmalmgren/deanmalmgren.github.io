## The situation

When I joined Planet FWD as CTO in early 2023, the product was four separate login properties sitting on more than 20,000 databases on an aging .NET stack. The engineering team worked in isolation: show up at stand-up, then disappear into separate corners. Work fell through the cracks and delivery was chronically late.

## What I did

- **Listened first.** I talked to every person in the company about the product and the dev team, then shared what I heard with the CEO, the leadership team, and the engineers.
- **Set a vision people wanted to build.** One integrated LCA and carbon accounting platform with a modern UX. Nothing like it existed in the market.
- **Made delivery predictable.** I moved the team to agile. First I had to reset what “agile” meant, because everyone had a different definition. Then I made a few key hires and built a culture where the team goal came before individual wins.
- **Picked a date and held it.** I set October 1, 2023 as the launch at Sustainable Brands. The leadership team wasn’t sure we could hit it. I argued it was achievable and mattered: we had media and a booth lined up, and companies set next year’s budgets in Q4.
- **Joined science to engineering.** The science and engineering teams now share a daily stand-up and build features together.
- **Built an operating cadence.** Every other week, a “boulders” meeting with sales and leadership picks the big epics, and a “rocks and pebbles” meeting handles smaller improvements. Both reforecast delivery, so nobody is caught off guard.

## What happened

- First results shipped within two months.
- We hit the October 1 launch and every intermediate deadline before it, then had the corporate inventory platform ready for inventory season in March 2024.
- Within about a year, four products and 20k+ databases became one product, one database, and one login. We retired the old platform once legacy customers had moved over.
- The product team grew from 4 to 12. Revenue has grown about 35% a year since I joined, and the company has reached cash-flow breakeven. Renewal revenue retention has been above 100% three years running, and I helped raise a Series A+ of more than $5M.

## What I’d tell you

Most rebuilds stall because teams try to fix the platform and the operating model separately. Fix them together, set a date people can rally around, and make delivery visible to the whole company.
