## The situation

Elemetric spun out of Datascope to make survey analysis less laborious. It grew out of [catcorr.js](https://ideo.github.io/catcorrjs/), an open source tool for exploring survey data.

## What I did

- As technical co-founder, I turned catcorr.js into a next-generation survey analysis product that automatically highlighted the most significant relationships in the data.
- We considered several other opportunities but chose Elemetric because it was what the team was most excited about. Enthusiasm counts for a lot when you spin off a company, but it meant betting everything on one idea.

## What happened

- We were accepted to Techstars in summer 2017, and that’s where we found product-market fit problems we couldn’t solve. We shut the company down.
- In hindsight, we built the product before we had proven the demand.

## What I’d tell you

Killing ideas quickly is one of the most important lessons I’ve learned. It’s why my zero-to-one work starts with the cheapest test of demand, not the product ([see the photo-to-LCA example](/work/photo-to-lca/)). And it’s why I like running several bets at once instead of one.
